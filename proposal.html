⚠️ Problem Statement

Current Challenges in School Management
  • Lost or damaged student records and transcripts
  • Delayed access to marks and attendance data
  • No centralized system for data management
  • Time-consuming administrative paperwork
  • Difficulty tracking student progress

💡 Proposed Solution

MSPCSS - Complete Digital School Management System
Admin Portal
Full control: Add/Delete Students & Teachers, Upload Files, Reports
Teacher Portal
Add Marks, Take Attendance, View Students, Export Excel
Student Portal
View Marks, Attendance, Subjects, Profile, Download QR
QR Login
Secure login using ID card QR code scanning

✨ Key Features (Part 1)

  • Complete Authentication System (Admin/Teacher/Student roles)
  • Student Management - Add, edit, delete students
  • Grade & Department Selection (Natural/Social for Grade 11-12)
  • Teacher Management with profile and documents
  • Marks Recording (Quiz, Midterm, Final, Assignment)
  • Attendance Tracking (Present/Absent/Late)

✨ Key Features (Part 2)

  • File Upload System (Transcripts, Certificates, Documents)
  • QR Code Generation & Download for Students/Teachers
  • QR Scanner Login using Camera
  • Excel Export for Marks and Attendance
  • Profile Pictures Upload
  • Data Persistence (localStorage - no database needed)
